Core Specializations

Research Themes

Past Fire Regimes

Using macro-charcoal and polycyclic aromatic hydrocarbon (PAH) biomarkers from sedimentary archives and archaeological sites to reconstruct prehistoric fire usage and natural palaeofire events.

Macro-Charcoals PAHs Belan Valley

Past Climate & Vegetation

Compound-specific carbon (δ13C) and hydrogen (δ2H) stable isotope analysis of terrestrial plant leaf-wax n-alkanes to trace Quaternary climate variability and vegetation shifts.

n-Alkanes δ13C & δ2H Monsoon

Human-Environment Dynamics

Transdisciplinary investigation into climate influences on hominin evolution, Acheulean habitations, Toba super-eruption environmental impacts, and Palaeolithic-to-Neolithic transitions.

Toba Eruption Acheulean Hominin Evolution
