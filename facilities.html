Instrumentation

Laboratory Facilities

Analytical instrumentation and field monitoring infrastructure supporting biomarker and stable isotope research at BASIL.

Gas Chromatograph – Mass Spectrometer

Being established

Purchase and installation funded by the ANRF PMECRG research grant. The GC-MS will support identification and quantification of leaf-wax n-alkanes, polycyclic aromatic hydrocarbons (PAHs) and other molecular biomarkers extracted from sediments and archaeological deposits.

Automated Rain Gauge Station

Operational

Hosted on campus to monitor monsoon variability and to collect precipitation for isotopic analysis, providing a modern reference dataset against which palaeo-hydrological reconstructions can be calibrated.

Researchers interested in collaborative access or sample analysis are welcome to get in touch.
