Biogeochemical Geoarchaeology Research Group

Department of Applied Geology, Indian Institute of Technology (ISM) Dhanbad

BASIL is a research group in the Department of Applied Geology at IIT (ISM) Dhanbad, working at the meeting point of organic geochemistry, Quaternary science and archaeology.

We work with molecular biomarkers preserved in sediments and archaeological deposits — terrestrial plant leaf-wax n-alkanes, macro-charcoal and polycyclic aromatic hydrocarbons — to reconstruct past environments, vegetation change and human fire use across South Asia. The group brings together doctoral researchers, master’s students, interns and collaborators in India and Europe.
